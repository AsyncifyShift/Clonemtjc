Welcome

Mother Teressa High School was established in the year 1983-84. M.T.H.S is recognised by the Maharashtra State Board Of Education. It aims at giving all-round sound education academically, morally, spiritually and culturally. The medium of instruction is strictly English. We ensure you that you can entrust your children in this institution for best guidance in character & morals.
